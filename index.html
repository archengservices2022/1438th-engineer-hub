Security & Data Rules

Allowed for this prototype

General public/non-sensitive reminders, drill dates already approved for sharing, packing reminders, public resource links, and generic administrative tasks.

Do NOT store

  • CUI or classified information
  • SSNs, DoD IDs, CAC credentials, passwords or access tokens
  • Medical records or individual readiness details
  • Detailed personnel rosters or sensitive PII
  • Weapons, tactical, operational, movement or mission-sensitive information
A real unit rollout needs approved identity/authentication, centralized access control, audit logging, encrypted backend storage, retention rules, and leadership/IT/security approval.
